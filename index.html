about

i've always been fascinated by the way mathematics seems to exist beneath everything. the more physics i learn, the more it feels like we're uncovering a language that was already there long before us. every equation, every proof, every strange result is another glimpse into something much larger.

most of my time is spent studying physics, teaching, and building resources that make difficult ideas a little more intuitive. i don't think understanding should feel reserved for a handful of people. with enough curiosity, patience, and the right explanation, almost anything can become clear.

i'm still learning. i probably always will be. that's what makes it exciting. every answer seems to uncover a better question.
